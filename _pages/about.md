I obtained the Master's degree supervised by Professor [Long Jin](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=zt5LfSeWy9gC&hl=zh-CN), majoring in Computer Technology with the School of Information Science and Engineering, [Lanzhou University](https://en.lzu.edu.cn/), Lanzhou, China, in 2023. I used to worked as a internship at Tencent, Robotics X, supervised by [Yu Zheng](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=oBXTTesAAAAJ&hl=en). My main research interests include robotics, artifical intelligence, security, cyber-physical systems, and medical imaging.
